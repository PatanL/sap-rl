**How to read the client's numbers.** Two things were learned about the display and are used throughout.

- The client shows health below zero for a pet that has just died, so overkill is readable.
- **Label lag.** When a pet is hit or buffed twice in quick succession (a Tiger repeat, two Wolverines, two Mammoth buffs), the label shows the first change only, until that pet's next change. In test f2x the Tiger shows 18 after two 2-damage blasts and 12 after the next 4-damage hit, so it was at 16. A terminal frame can therefore be one change stale; where it disagrees with the archived ResultingBoard, the archive is right. This explains session 2's one mismatch (fight 6b2e1d99: client 8/9, archive 8/8). `pair3.py` reports a strict first difference and one that tolerates a stale label; every rule below rests on a difference that survives the tolerant reading or on arithmetic at the pet's next change.
