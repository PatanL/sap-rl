The September configurations give **2e-6 base, 1e-4 opponent-history, 1e-5 opponent-adapter, 5e-5 END/freeze**, with 8,192 learner decisions per update. Training logs label half the match-sampling probability `main`. The other half was distributed among an expert anchor, four seeded older policies (v31 integration 1,000, v25 300, v22 2,000 and v44 control 3,250), and up to four rotating historical snapshots. Opponents with a low estimated learner win rate received greater sampling weight. This is a mixture by new matches; it does not imply half the decision rows came from each source. At the end of the 20,806 phase the logged probabilities were 50% main, about 17.5% combined fixed/expert and 32.5% recent historical. The initial student also used search-target replay, and later phases introduced opening-temperature exploration; those variables confound causal attribution to either opponents or LR. The 20,806→25,772 phase resumed real Adam and the league, with the search replay exhausted. **November:** the population-league continuation at the same base rate scored 52.60% on its 1,024-update monitor but 49.375% [46.25, 52.66] in confirmation. A small base step is a plausible contributor. There is no matched rate-only experiment here, and the lack of reliable immediate-parent gains already appears in September. Pure mirrors, the change in engine, half-size update batches, exploration, historical diversity and behavior-policy lag all change the interpretation of the live v31 trial. [November result](../population-league-25772-v1/result.json) · [November config](../population-league-25772-v1/training/config.json)

**Rate recommendation after the first live score.** Restart the proposed comparison from 25,772 at the saved base 2e-6 with its original head rates, then test base 5e-6 alone over an equal accepted-decision budget if progress remains flat. I would not spend the full 20,000 updates on the present settings. The 5× treatment already regressed on the screen and an independent confirmation. It raised all four rates (base 1e-5, history 5e-4, adapter 5e-5, END/freeze 2.5e-4), used only live self-play and changed the data regime. Confidence is high that this tested checkpoint is worse under greedy play, moderate in returning to the known reference settings, and low about which change caused the regression. The new launcher’s `--base-lr` option permits a base-only comparison.

**Opponent recommendation.** For the next controlled continuation, use 50% live mirrors, 25% against frozen 25,772 and 25% uniformly across the latest four saved policies, learning only from the live seat in frozen matches. Until a snapshot exists, allocate its share to 25,772. Confidence is moderate for anchoring half the games, lower for this exact parent/snapshot split. It captures temporal diversity at modest complexity and can be compared against the ongoing pure-mirror control without modifying it.
